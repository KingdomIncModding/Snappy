# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- Requires Foundation 0.11.0 or newer.

## 1.1.0

- Added a free Snappy research unlock under Tech Tree > Mods.
- Fine snapping is enabled separately for each kingdom after researching Snappy.
- Introduced a Foundation dependency.

## 1.0.0

- Initial release with three extra snap positions between original grid points.
- Applied finer snapping to node placement, copy previews, pipe bends, and decorations.
- Retained the usual Ctrl free-placement behavior.
