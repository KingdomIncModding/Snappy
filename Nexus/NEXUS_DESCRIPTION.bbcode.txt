[b]Snappy[/b]

Adds three evenly spaced snap positions between each original grid position on both axes. A 64-unit interval becomes four 16-unit intervals: 0, 16, 32, 48, 64.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Finer placement for nodes, pipe bends and decorations.[/*]
[*]Works with dragging, copy previews and container resizing.[/*]
[*]Hold Ctrl for free placement.[/*]
[*]Free research unlock, saved per kingdom.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]
Kingdom Inc, BepInEx 5 and Foundation 0.5.0 or later.

[b]Installation and use[/b]
1. Install the requirements.
2. Close Kingdom Inc.
3. Extract the ZIP into the game folder. The plugin belongs at BepInEx/plugins/Snappy/Snappy.dll.
4. Launch the game and open Tech Tree → Mods.
5. Research Snappy for free to enable finer snapping.

The normal grid remains active until Snappy is researched. Each kingdom has its own unlock.

[b]Updating[/b]
Close the game and replace Snappy.dll. When updating from 1.0.0, research Snappy for free to enable finer snapping. Existing placements are preserved.

[b]Placement[/b]
The visible background grid keeps its original spacing. Extra snap positions lie between its lines. Node sizes, connection counts and minimum container size remain unchanged. Gallery dots illustrate positions; Snappy does not draw a marker overlay.

[b]Removal[/b]
Close the game and delete BepInEx/plugins/Snappy. Existing positions remain intact; subsequent dragging uses the normal grid. Saving without Snappy may discard its research unlock, requiring the free research again after reinstalling.
