# Prerequisites

| Required Mod | Download | Minimum Version | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | [Foundation on Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/kingdominc/mods/18) | 0.11.0 | Provides the mod research integration used by Snappy. |
| BepInEx 5 (Windows Mono) | [Official BepInEx releases](https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases) | 5.x | Required to load Snappy and Foundation. |

Also requires **Kingdom Inc** for Windows (Unity Mono).

Foundation is a required Nexus mod and should be linked in the Nexus page's **Requirements** section, not just mentioned in the description. BepInEx is an off-site requirement.
